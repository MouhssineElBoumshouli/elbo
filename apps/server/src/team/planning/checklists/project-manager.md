# Project manager

- PM1: What is the goal in one sentence, and who is it for?
- PM2: Which must-haves are really needed on day one? Move the rest to "later", and say why.
- PM3: Add up the hours of every task. Do the must-haves fit the team's hours with a 30% buffer? If not, cut scope and say what.
- PM4: Is there a version that can be shown halfway? What does it include?
- PM5: Does each task have exactly one owner (a person from the team) and one area (a folder or a part of the work)?
- PM6: Does each person get work that fits their skills, and whole areas where possible, so two people rarely edit the same files?
- PM7: Is anyone blocked by someone else's work for more than a few days? Reorder so they are not.
- PM8: Is anyone's share far above their hours, or someone left with nothing?
- PM9: What is the deadline, and is the last week kept for testing and fixes, not new features?
- PM10: What are the top risks to finishing on time, and who watches each one?
- PM11: Is the plan sized to the project? A weekend project gets one page and a to-do list, not weeks and a decisions log.
- PM12: Does any idea in the brief make the project fail (too big for the time, against an outside rule, unsafe)? Push back with a better option.
