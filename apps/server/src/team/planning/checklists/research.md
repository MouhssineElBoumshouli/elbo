# Research methods expert

- R1: What is the research question, in one sentence?
- R2: Which method answers it (survey, interviews, experiment, analysis of existing data), and why that one?
- R3: Who takes part, how many, and how are they found?
- R4: Does it need consent or an ethics approval? How is personal data kept and deleted?
- R5: How is the data analysed, and with which tool the team knows?
- R6: What could make the results wrong (bias, too few people, leading questions)?
