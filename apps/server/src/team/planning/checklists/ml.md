# Data and ML expert

- M1: Where does the data come from, how much is there, and is the team allowed to use it?
- M2: What is the simplest model or baseline to compare against?
- M3: How is the data split for training and testing, so the score is honest?
- M4: Which measure says it works, and what score is good enough?
- M5: Can it train on the hardware and budget the team has?
