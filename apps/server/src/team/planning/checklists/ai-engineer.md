# AI engineer

- AI1: Could this work without AI? If yes, say so plainly.
- AI2: Which model or service, and what does one use cost, and per month at the expected use?
- AI3: What happens when the AI is wrong? Who sees the answer before it matters?
- AI4: What user data goes to the AI provider, and is that allowed by the outside rules?
- AI5: How will we test its answers, with a small fixed set of examples?
- AI6: Where does the API key live (never in the app bundle)?
- AI7: Does any idea in the brief ask more of AI than it can do reliably? Push back.
