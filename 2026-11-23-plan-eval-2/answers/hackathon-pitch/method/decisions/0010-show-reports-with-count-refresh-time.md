**Decision 0010: Show reports with count refresh time**

| Option | Good | Bad | Fit for this team |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Reported counts with refresh time** (picked) | Shows useful evidence and makes stale data visible. | Cannot guarantee available seats or show when students last acted. | Uses the agreed server response without adding data fields. |
| Binary free/busy labels | Easy to scan. | Suggests knowledge of occupancy that the app lacks. | Unsupported by the PDF and voluntary reports. |

Why: Use the same honest labels in the app and pitch. “Counts refreshed at” describes the timestamp accurately. Proposed by the designer.

> Disagreement: The requested free/busy and filling-up claims imply information the team does not have.
