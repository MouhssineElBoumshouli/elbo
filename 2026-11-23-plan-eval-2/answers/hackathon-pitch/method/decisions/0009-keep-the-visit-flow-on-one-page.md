**Decision 0009: Keep the visit flow on one page**

| Option | Good | Bad | Fit for this team |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Room cards and embedded My check-in** (picked) | Keeps actions visible and reduces navigation. | Leaves less space for future room information. | Fits Noor’s three design hours and Tom’s existing frontend allocation. |
| Separate Rooms, Details and My check-in views | Provides room for richer information later. | Adds navigation and more states to design and test. | Unnecessary for names, buildings and check-ins at this deadline. |

Why: The available room information fits on cards, and the active visit stays easy to find. Proposed by the designer.
